[SLIDE CONTENT - COPY & PASTE]
Slide Title:
  DEFINING INSIGHTS · USER NEEDS
Subtitle:
  Synthesizing Field Research into Actionable Functional Requirements

1. PRIMARY NEEDS (Core Prerequisites):
  - Real-Time Cabin Presence: Students need to know if the professor is physically 'Available', 'In Class', 'In Meeting', or 'Busy'.
  - Micro-Interaction Simplicity for Faculty: Changing availability must take less than 2 seconds; otherwise, professors will abandon the tool.
  - Zero Barrier to Entry for Students: Must not force students to download a 60MB native app or undergo complex registration.

2. SECONDARY NEEDS (Operational Efficiency):
  - Digital Consultation Booking: Structured 15-minute slots for project reviews, doubt clarification, and approvals.
  - Cabin Location & Navigation Context: Clear room numbering (e.g., PG-302, 3rd Floor) and cabin directions.
  - Queue Transparency: Knowing how many students are already waiting outside to avoid physical crowding.

3. LATENT NEEDS (Unspoken Psychological Factors):
  - Boundary Protection: Professors need a polite, systematic way to signal 'Do Not Disturb' without seeming inaccessible.
  - Network Resilience: The application must function reliably over high-density campus Wi-Fi and intermittent 4G/5G signals.

[PRESENTER'S SPOKEN SCRIPT]
  "By analyzing our empathy data, we categorized user needs into Primary, Secondary, and Latent needs. Most notably, we discovered a vital latent need: professors desperately wanted boundaries without appearing rude, and students wanted confirmation without having to install heavy third-party apps on their phones."

Slide Title:
  IDEATION METHOD · SCAMPER FRAMEWORK
Subtitle:
  Structured Divergent & Convergent Thinking to Reinvent the Door Placard

SCAMPER Breakdown:
  - S (Substitute):
    * Replaced fragile paper sticky notes and static nameplates with real-time digital placards connected to a cloud database.
  - C (Combine):
    * Combined physical wall signage with dynamic QR codes, instant mobile queuing, and calendar slot booking.
  - A (Adapt):
    * Adapted the concept of hospital emergency triage displays and airport departure boards for university academic cabins.
  - M (Modify / Magnify):
    * Simplified scheduling into a 1-tap status selector (Available, Busy, In Meeting, In Class) taking under 1 second.
  - P (Put to Other Uses):
    * Repurposed the empty space beside cabin doors into an interactive digital touchpoint for students.
  - E (Eliminate):
    * Eliminated corridor loitering, awkward knocks, and intrusive WhatsApp messaging.
  - R (Reverse / Reorganize):
    * Reversed the paradigm: Instead of students searching blindly for faculty, faculty broadcast their status dynamically to all students.

  TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY & ARCHITECTURE
Subtitle:
  High-Performance, Fault-Tolerant Full-Stack Architecture Built for Campus Networks

Key Engineering Pillars:
  - Frontend Client Tier:
    * React 19 + TypeScript + Vite for ultra-fast, lightweight bundle size (<90 KB gzipped).
    * Progressive Web App (PWA) with HTML5 Camera QR scanner and responsive mobile styling.
  - Real-Time Communication Tier:
    * Server-Sent Events (SSE) connection hub providing persistent unidirectional streaming.
    * Adaptive Fallback Engine: Automatically detects mobile screen sleep (`visibilitychange`) and triggers intelligent 2-second polling if network drops.
  - Backend Engine Tier:
    * Node.js & Express.js REST API with structured event broadcast channels.
    * Zero-dependency cryptographic QR code generator.
  - Relational Database Tier:
    * PostgreSQL 16 relational database with Prisma ORM ensuring strict schema validation and ACID transactions.
    * Seeded campus dataset: 23 Faculty members, 18 Cabins, across 5 VNR VJIET departmental blocks.
