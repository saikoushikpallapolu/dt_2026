* **3. Latent Needs (Unspoken Psychological Factors):**
  * *Polite Boundary Enforcement:* Faculty need a polite, systemic way to say 'Do Not Disturb' without looking inaccessible.
  * *Campus Network Resilience:* Reliable performance over congested college Wi-Fi and mobile data.

### Content to Copy & Paste
* **Slide Title:** **IDEATION METHOD · SCAMPER FRAMEWORK**
* **Subtitle:** Systematic Innovation Framework Applied to University Scheduling
* **SCAMPER Elements:**
  * **S (Substitute):** Substituted static paper door placards with dynamic digital placards connected to a cloud database.
  * **C (Combine):** Combined physical door signage with dynamic QR codes, instant mobile queuing, and calendar slot booking.
  * **A (Adapt):** Adapted the concept of hospital triage displays and airport flight departure status boards for university cabins.
  * **M (Modify):** Simplified scheduling into a 1-tap quick status selector (Available, Busy, In Meeting, In Class) taking <1 second.
  * **P (Put to Other Uses):** Repurposed empty hallway door spaces into interactive digital touchpoints for students.
  * **E (Eliminate):** Eliminated corridor loitering, awkward knocks, and intrusive WhatsApp messaging.
  * **R (Reverse):** Reversed the paradigm: Instead of students searching blindly for faculty, faculty broadcast their status dynamically to all students.

## Slide 12: Technical Feasibility & System Architecture

### Visual Blueprint & Layout
* **Layout:** Central placement of the system architecture diagram (`diagram_system_architecture.png`) with 4 bulleted technical callouts.

### Content to Copy & Paste
* **Slide Title:** **TECHNICAL FEASIBILITY & ARCHITECTURE**
* **Subtitle:** High-Performance, Fault-Tolerant Full-Stack Architecture Built for Campus Networks
* **Architecture Highlights:**
  * **Client Tier (PWA & Web):** React 19 + TypeScript + Vite for an ultra-lean bundle size (<90 KB gzipped) with responsive mobile design.
  * **Real-Time Engine Tier (SSE):** Server-Sent Events (SSE) hub providing continuous, battery-efficient status streaming (<150ms latency).
  * **Adaptive Sync Manager:** Automatically detects phone screen sleep (`visibilitychange`) and triggers 2-second background polling fallback to overcome campus Wi-Fi drops.
  * **Backend & Data Tier:** Node.js & Express.js REST API coupled with PostgreSQL 16 relational database via Prisma ORM for type-safe ACID transactions.
  * **Campus Seed Dataset:** 23 Faculty members, 18 Cabins, across 5 VNR VJIET departmental blocks.
